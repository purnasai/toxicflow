## Findings

- I checked prior findings for `web`, `dispatcher`, `notifier`, and `records`; 
none were recorded.
- The per-agent scan found no agent toxic on its own: `web` has 
`untrusted_input`, `records` has `private_read`, and `notifier` has 
`private_read` plus `exfil_sink`. The multi-hop scan found **two toxic flows**; 
neither was `UNKNOWN`.

## Most dangerous flow

Untrusted content from `github.get_issue` at `web` can travel via `dispatcher` 
to `notifier`, which reads private data with `memory.read_graph` and can send it
out using `slack.slack_post_message`. The scan also found a route involving 
`records` and its private `filesystem.read_text_file` data, with the same 
delegated path: `web` → `dispatcher` → `notifier`. The shared `memory/notes` 
store is written by `web` and read by `notifier`.

## Repair proposed

I proposed isolating `notifier` from `memory/notes` and making `web` return 
structured results only to `dispatcher`. Simulation confirmed **no remaining 
flows and no broken tasks**. The proposal is pending human approval.

Obvious single fixes are insufficient: making only `web` structured-only leaves 
both flows, while making only `records` structured-only leaves one. Revoking key
read or send capabilities, or removing required delegations, breaks tasks.
